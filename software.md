# Developer Software and Infrastructure

Tools and services for building, deploying, securing and operating software. Items can appear in multiple categories when the catalog assigns more than one use.

## Legal & compliance

- [eNotas](https://enotas.com.br/) — Automate Brazilian tax-invoice issuance and connect it to your sales system through an API. Useful for SaaS billing operations. Supported document types, municipal rules and tax setup must be checked; this does not replace an accountant. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=eNotas).
- [Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/) — Use AI to research legal questions, draft documents and review contracts. Built for law firms and in-house legal teams, with shared document workspaces and integrations. Results still need professional review. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Harvey).

## Writing & editing

- [Lunchbreak AI](https://lunchbreak.ai/) — Check drafts for AI-detection flags and rewrite awkward wording. Useful for reviewing articles, emails and product copy before publishing. Detector scores do not prove who wrote a text or guarantee acceptance. Free checks require an account; rewriting has paid word limits. Uploaded text is stored and sent to detection services. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Lunchbreak%20AI).

## Careers & resumes

- [Enhancv](https://enhancv.com/) — Build a resume, tailor it to a job description and organize applications. Helps developers present projects, skills and work experience more clearly, with AI writing suggestions and resume checks. A limited seven-day free plan is available; continued Pro features require payment. Match scores do not guarantee interviews or a job. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Enhancv).

## Market research

- [GatherHQ](https://www.gatherhq.com/) — Research what customers need through interviews, existing feedback and AI simulations. Helps teams test messaging and product ideas. Developers can connect the research to agents through MCP or a REST API. Simulations are estimates, not new interviews. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=GatherHQ).
- [NoctralSpy · Noctral](https://noctralspy.com/) — Research competing mobile apps and web SaaS before building your product. Compare estimated revenue, downloads, rankings, reviews and ads to choose a niche or improve an existing app. Revenue figures are estimates or publicly reported amounts, not audited accounts or guaranteed demand. Paid web service; confirm the current offer at checkout. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=NoctralSpy%20%C2%B7%20Noctral).
- [ScrapeCreators](https://scrapecreators.com/) — Fetch public social posts, profiles, comments and advertising data through an API. Helps developers build research dashboards without maintaining every scraper. Uses prepaid credits; external sites can block endpoints, and the terms allow credits to expire. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=ScrapeCreators).

Requires telephony setup and usage credits; it is not an AI assistant by itself. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=API4COM).
- [Fluke](https://flu.ke/) — Fluke is a Brazilian mobile operator with a dashboard for buying company lines, assigning them to staff and checking usage. Offers SIM/eSIM lines and separate virtual numbers for WhatsApp Business. Helps developers equip support and chatbot operations with numbers and automate virtual-line management using an API and webhooks. Check activation and monthly charges. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Fluke).
- [Kapso](https://kapso.com/) — Add WhatsApp Business messaging to your product with an API, webhooks and a shared inbox. Helps developers connect customer numbers, automate conversations and give agents WhatsApp tools through MCP. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Kapso).
- [Novu](https://www.novu.co/) — Send product notifications and agent messages across inbox, email, SMS, push and chat channels. Helps developers manage templates, preferences and delivery workflows in one place. MIT core, proprietary Enterprise modules and separate cloud plans; sending-provider charges and channel limits still apply. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Novu).
- [Photon](https://photon.codes/) — Connect apps and AI agents to messaging channels through a unified API, including iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram and Slack. Helps developers send and receive conversations and media without building each connector from scratch. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Photon).
- [Salvy](https://salvy.com.br/) — Manage company mobile lines, SIMs and eSIMs from one dashboard. For developers, Salvy also provides virtual numbers for WhatsApp Business activation, with verification codes delivered by webhook. That virtual-number product is WhatsApp-only and does not include calls or mobile data. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Salvy).
- [StackZap](https://stackzap.com.br/) — Connect WhatsApp to your app, CRM, Chatwoot or AI agent using REST APIs, webhooks and MCP. Paid Cloud hosting provides a separate API and database per customer, with connection and storage limits. Independent integration, not Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=StackZap).
- [Tidio](https://www.tidio.com/) — Customer service software combining live chat, help-desk tickets, automated flows and Lyro AI agents for support conversations. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Tidio).
- [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/) — Communication APIs for SMS, WhatsApp, voice calls and email, plus tools for customer engagement and conversational AI. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Twilio).
- [Vapi](https://vapi.ai/) — Build voice agents that make and receive calls or talk inside your app. Connect your own tools and choose speech, model and voice providers. Costs can include those providers and telephony, beyond the platform charge. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=Vapi).
- [wacli](https://wacli.sh/) — wacli is a free, open-source WhatsApp command-line tool for syncing chats to a local SQLite database, searching message history offline, and managing messages, media, contacts and groups from scripts or AI agents. It pairs as a linked WhatsApp Web device; history is best-effort, and it is an unofficial third-party tool. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=wacli).
- [WAHA](https://waha.devlike.pro/) — Self-host a WhatsApp HTTP API to send messages and receive events in your backend. Apache-2.0; former Plus features are included in Core from 2026.6.1. This is not Meta Cloud API: you maintain account sessions, and WhatsApp can restrict them. [Radar Scout](https://my-tierroom.party/software?q=WAHA).
